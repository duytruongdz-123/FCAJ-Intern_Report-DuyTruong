Welcome to my Worklog. This document chronicles the entire journey of researching, deploying, and optimizing a monitoring system over the course of the three-month (12-week) project. My focus was entirely on building, operating, and managing performance measurement and alerting systems within a cloud computing environment.

The work roadmap was designed to progress from fundamental concepts to advanced implementation: starting with an introduction to core AWS services, then moving on to the in-depth setup and integration of a comprehensive monitoring system using two key tools, Amazon CloudWatch and Grafana.
